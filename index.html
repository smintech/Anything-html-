Learn to Jump Rope!

Swing, jump, repeat!
1
👉
👉

Hold Handles

Hold handles at your hips! Keep them steady.

2

Swing Rope

Swing from your wrists making small circles!

3

Jump Lightly

Small jumps on the balls of your feet. Soft landing!

4

Find Rhythm

Jump when the rope hits the ground! Tick-tock!

5

Keep Trying!

Missed? Start again! Practice makes progress!

🗣️
Say "jump, jump, jump" out loud to keep your time!
❤️
Jump rope builds strong legs and a very happy heart!
